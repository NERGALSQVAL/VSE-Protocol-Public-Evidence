# Regulatory Position

## Technical Verification ≠ Regulatory Recognition

VSE Protocol is currently positioned as **machine-verifiable renewable-energy evidence and data infrastructure**.

VSE does not claim, solely by operation of the software, to be:

- an official Guarantee of Origin (`GO`);
- an EECS certificate;
- a government-issued renewable-energy certificate;
- an electricity-trading authorization;
- automatically accepted for Scope 2 reporting;
- automatically recognized under every jurisdiction or corporate reporting framework.

Buyer acceptance, regulatory treatment, reporting eligibility, accounting treatment, and assurance requirements depend on the applicable legal, contractual, technical, and reporting framework.

## External Review

External security, legal, smart-contract, or assurance reviews may assess the implementation or operating environment.

They do **not** determine or override the mathematical validity of an individual VSE proof.

A failed validation remains failed.
