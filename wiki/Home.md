## Welcome

This wiki is the public technical knowledge base for VSE Protocol.

VSE is designed to transform authenticated renewable-energy production data into deterministic, auditable, single-use digital proofs.

```text
PHYSICAL ENERGY ASSET
        ↓
MANUFACTURER-AUTHORITATIVE DATA
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
AUDIT CHAIN
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
RETIREMENT
        ↓

```
