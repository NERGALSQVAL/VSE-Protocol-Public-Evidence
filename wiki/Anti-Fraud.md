# Anti-Fraud

<div align="center">

## I AM THE MATH.

</div>

The VSE Anti-Fraud model is based on deterministic machine validation rather than human approval.

## Core Rules

- authoritative machine source;
- raw evidence first;
- source identity matching;
- cryptographic integrity;
- deterministic energy calculation;
- time integrity;
- duplicate and double-use prevention;
- no human override of failed evidence;
- append-only audit history;
- no retroactive manipulation;
- automated source exclusion where required;
- administrators cannot create energy;
- commercial relationships do not affect validity;
- retirement is final;
- no fabricated live state.

## No Commercial Override

Buyer size, contract value, subscription level, partnership status, or commercial importance do not change mathematical validation.

> **ONE PROTOCOL. SAME MATHEMATICS FOR EVERYONE.**

## No Admin Override

Administrative users may manage operational systems, support, billing, access, organizations, and review workflows. They must not fabricate production, bypass duplicate protection, mint unsupported energy, or convert failed evidence into valid evidence.

## Audit Rule

> **NEW INFORMATION CREATES NEW STATE. IT DOES NOT REWRITE OLD STATE.**

## Final Principle

> **CODE DOESN'T HAVE POCKETS.**
