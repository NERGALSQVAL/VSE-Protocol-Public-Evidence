# VSE Unit & Retirement

## VSE Unit

A VSE Unit is a verification instrument representing accepted renewable-energy state.

It is not designed as a freely tradable cryptocurrency.

## Intended Registry Rules

- authorized VSE minting only;
- VSE-controlled registry ownership;
- no unrestricted public transfer;
- no open secondary market;
- no arbitrary approval mechanism;
- source / audit / interval duplicate prevention;
- one-time retirement;
- no reactivation after final retirement.

## Retirement

Retirement represents final use or allocation of a verified VSE Unit.

A finally retired unit must not:

- return to available inventory;
- be sold again;
- be retired twice;
- be reactivated;
- be silently reassigned to another beneficiary.

A retirement record may bind the unit to a beneficiary, purpose, sale or allocation, methodology, protocol version, and blockchain transaction.

> **RETIREMENT IS FINAL.**

## Blockchain Role

Blockchain state supports registry finality and proof verification. It is not the original source of the renewable-energy measurement.
