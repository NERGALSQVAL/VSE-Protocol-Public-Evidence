## Purpose

This document defines the technical admission and validation rules of the VSE Protocol network. These rules are not marketing language and are not intended to be negotiable commercial policy.

```text
SOURCE → RAW EVIDENCE → HASH → VALIDATION → ENERGY CALCULATION → AUDIT CHAIN → MINT → RETIREMENT → PROOF
```

---

# 01 // No Human Override

VSE follows one protocol and one mathematical validation model. No privileged buyer, administrator, partner, producer, or commercial account may force failed energy evidence into the verified state.

> **ADMINISTRATIVE PRIVILEGE ENDS WHERE MATHEMATICAL VERIFICATION BEGINS.**

Human review may exist for support, investigation, compliance, security, legal review, and enterprise assurance. It must not alter raw evidence or convert failed mathematical validation into valid energy.

---

# 02 // Authoritative Machine Source

VSE accepts production evidence only through an approved machine-authoritative source associated with an identifiable physical energy asset.

```text
PHYSICAL DEVICE
      ↓
MANUFACTURER INFRASTRUCTURE
      ↓
VSE VERIFICATION ENGINE
```

Manual values, spreadsheets, screenshots, human declarations, edited payloads, and unverifiable production totals are rejected as primary authoritative evidence.

---

# 03 // Source Identity Must Match

Validation may include plant ID, inverter ID, logger ID, meter ID, authorization context, source metadata, production interval, and source timestamps.

Evidence that cannot be associated with the expected physical source must not create verified energy.

---

# 04 // Raw Evidence First

The original manufacturer response is captured before verified state is created.

```text
SOURCE RESPONSE
      ↓
RAW EVIDENCE
      ↓
CRYPTOGRAPHIC HASH
      ↓
VALIDATION
      ↓
VSE UNIT
```

No proof may be created first and justified with evidence later.

---

# 05 // Cryptographic Integrity

VSE independently fingerprints received evidence. A hash is not accepted as correct merely because an external provider supplied it.

Where available, authenticated requests, signatures, HMAC values, or equivalent cryptographic integrity controls may also be verified.

---

# 06 // Deterministic Energy Calculation

VSE calculates energy according to the active methodology using validated source evidence.

> **The same valid evidence processed under the same methodology and protocol version should produce the same result.**

No administrator or commercial actor may manually alter the mathematical result.

---

# 07 // Time Integrity

VSE may validate timestamps, interval order, missing samples, sampling gaps, future timestamps, duplicate intervals, and previously processed time ranges.

A production interval already accepted by the protocol must not become new verified inventory again.

---

# 08 // Zero Double Counting

Duplicate protection may operate across source identity, source evidence hash, production interval, audit identifier, VSE Unit identifier, registry state, and retirement state.

The same underlying energy must not be accepted again through another account, API key, organization, customer, or transaction.

---

# 09 // Immutable Audit History

Accepted verification events are appended to cryptographically linked history.

> **NEW INFORMATION CREATES NEW STATE. IT DOES NOT REWRITE OLD STATE.**

Corrections, exclusions, investigations, or security actions create new auditable events instead of silently rewriting accepted history.

---

# 10 // No Retroactive Manipulation

Historical accepted evidence must not be silently altered to improve commercial outcomes. If later evidence indicates manipulation, compromise, revocation, or source invalidity, VSE records a new state and may exclude the affected source from future participation.

---

# 11 // Automated Source Exclusion

A source may be blocked or excluded for authentication failure, authorization revocation, source identity mismatch, duplicate evidence, impossible timestamps, cryptographic mismatch, repeated invalid payloads, or detected manipulation.

---

# 12 // Administrators Cannot Create Energy

Administrative accounts may operate the platform but do not possess authority to invent renewable-energy production. They must not fabricate evidence, create unsupported verified state, bypass duplicate protection, or force a failed validation to pass.

---

# 13 // Commercial Relationships Do Not Affect Validation

Buyer size, contract value, subscription tier, strategic status, partnership, or commercial importance must not modify source-admission or mathematical-validation rules.

> **ONE PROTOCOL. SAME MATHEMATICS FOR EVERYONE.**

---

# 14 // VSE Unit Rules

A VSE Unit is a verification instrument, not a freely tradable speculative token.

- authorized VSE minting only;
- VSE-controlled registry ownership;
- no unrestricted public transfer;
- no open secondary market;
- no arbitrary approval mechanism;
- no duplicate source / audit / interval minting;
- one-time retirement;
- no reactivation after retirement.

---

# 15 // Retirement Is Final

A retired VSE Unit must not return to available inventory, be resold, be silently reassigned, be retired twice, or be reactivated.

Retirement creates the final single-use state of the verified energy unit.

---

# 16 // Machine-Verifiable Proof

A final VSE proof may expose or reference the VSE Unit ID, verified energy quantity, production interval, source evidence hash, audit hashes, methodology hash, mint transaction, retirement transaction, beneficiary hash, purpose hash, sale hash, smart-contract address, network identifier, protocol version, and VSE proof signature.

**The visual proof page represents the cryptographic state. The cryptographic state is the evidence.**

---

# 17 // No Silent Failure

Unavailable remains unavailable. Pending remains pending. Failed remains failed. Unverified remains unverified.

VSE must not fabricate manufacturer production, source confirmations, blockchain transactions, audit results, energy inventory, or retirement events.
