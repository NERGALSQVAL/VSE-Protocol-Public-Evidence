## What VSE Is

VSE Protocol is a renewable-energy verification infrastructure designed to transform authenticated machine telemetry into deterministic, auditable, single-use digital energy proofs.

The system is built around a simple idea:

> **A renewable-energy claim should be supported by evidence that software can independently verify.**

VSE connects physical renewable-energy production to a final proof through an evidence-first verification chain.

```text
PHYSICAL ENERGY ASSET
        ↓
MANUFACTURER-AUTHORITATIVE DATA
        ↓
RAW EVIDENCE
        ↓
CRYPTOGRAPHIC INTEGRITY
        ↓
DETERMINISTIC VALIDATION
        ↓
ENERGY CALCULATION
        ↓
AUDIT CHAIN
        ↓
VSE UNIT
        ↓
MINT
        ↓
ALLOCATION
        ↓
RETIREMENT
        ↓
MACHINE-VERIFIABLE PROOF
```

## Why It Exists

Traditional renewable-energy claims can depend heavily on documents, manual processes, intermediaries, and trust in whoever performed the verification.

VSE is designed to reduce that trust dependency by preserving the underlying machine evidence and making the resulting verification state independently checkable.

The protocol is intended to answer questions such as:

- Which physical source produced the energy?
- When was it produced?
- Where did the source data originate?
- Was the raw evidence preserved?
- Was the evidence modified?
- Was the same interval already used?
- Was the verified unit already retired?
- Can the final proof be independently checked?

## What VSE Is Not

VSE is **not** designed as a general-purpose cryptocurrency or speculative token project.

The VSE Unit is intended as a verification instrument used to preserve verified state and final retirement.

VSE also does not claim, solely by operation of the software, to be:

- an official Guarantee of Origin (`GO`);
- an EECS certificate;
- a government-issued renewable-energy certificate;
- an electricity-trading authorization;
- automatically accepted under every Scope 2 or corporate reporting framework.

Technical proof and regulatory recognition are separate questions.

## Evidence-First Architecture

VSE is designed around five non-negotiable ideas:

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Authoritative source** | Energy evidence must originate from an identifiable machine-authoritative source. |
| **Raw evidence first** | Original source data is preserved before verified state is created. |
| **Deterministic validation** | The same valid evidence under the same methodology should produce the same result. |
| **Append-only audit** | New information creates new state instead of silently rewriting old state. |
| **Final retirement** | Retired verified energy must not return to active inventory or be used twice. |
